## Email 3: Scam Avoidance (Day 4)
**Subject: 5 lies exit companies tell you (and how to spot them)**

Hi {first_name},

I've read through 500+ complaints against timeshare exit companies. The stories are all similar. Here are the 5 most common lies — and exactly how to spot the real ones
**Lie #1: "We guarantee your exit"**
No one can guarantee a timeshare exit. The developer makes the final decision. Legitimate companies will say "we help you pursue your exit" — not "we guarantee results."

**Lie #2: "We have a buyer waiting"**
If they had a buyer, they'd be selling timeshares — not exit fees. The resale value of most timeshares is zero. Anyone claiming otherwise is lying.

**Lie #3: "Pay us $5,000 and we'll handle everything"**
The actual legal work costs $200–$500. The other $4,500 is their sales commission. You can do most of this yourself.

**Lie #4: "Sign this form and we'll take care of it"**
Anytime someone asks you to sign documents you haven't read, walk away. Read everything. Understand everything. Your signature is the only real leverage you have.

**Lie #5: "We're endorsed by the government/FTC/Bars"**
The FTC doesn't endorse anyone. Check their website (ftc.gov) to verify any claimed endorsements.

**The red flag checklist** (from the playbook, page 23)
- Requests ANY money upfront → RUN
- Claims a "guarantee" → RUN
- Says they have a "buyer" → RUN
- Asks you to stop paying the resort → RUN
- Won't give references → RUN

Want to verify a company quickly? Google "[Company Name] + scam + complaint" — the results will tell you everything.
